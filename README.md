# Manatee Slugger: Crystal Springs Derby

A 10-pitch coastal lagoon home run derby starring Manny the Manatee and Penny the Pelican.

**Play live:** https://cloudsurgeon.github.io/manatee-slugger/

## Features

- **Easy & Classic Modes:** Choose **Easy Mode** (slower lobs, wider sweet spot, and a glowing `SWING!` cue) or **Classic Mode** (full pitch variety and classic timing) right on the start screen, with separate saved high scores.
- **iPad & Mobile Ready:** Touch-optimized with zero double-tap zoom, safe-area inset support, orientation resize handling, a dedicated thumb-friendly Swing bar on touch devices, and iOS Safari Web Audio unlock & interruption recovery.
- **Lagoon Targets:** Bounce off Pink Flamingos (+150), collect Seagrass Power (+200 + next-pitch power boost), and hit Golden Conches (+1 bonus pitch +300).

## Controls

- **Swing:** Tap anywhere on the lagoon, tap the Swing bar (mobile/iPad), click, or press `Space` / `↑`
- **Restart Round:** `R` or **New Round**
- **Toggle Sound:** `M` or **Sound: On/Off**
